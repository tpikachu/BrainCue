## M3 review round 6 (`feature/interview-copilot-first`)

Continues [2026-11-17](./2026-11-17.md) (M1–M3 and M3 review rounds 1–5).
Codex found two ordering defects:

- **A queued revision left stale metadata.** The queued-repeat branch of
  `routeKnown()` changed the wording only: `finalAt`, `decidedAt` and the
  continuation target stayed with the first wording. With B and C queued, a
  longer B2 then "And how you tested it" attached the follow-up to C; a
  revision 10 s after its question (behind a manual Ask) fell outside the
  continuation window it should have restarted.
- **Equal timestamps defeated the newest-question guard.** `setLast()`
  compared `finalAt` with `>=`, so an older queued question starting in the
  same millisecond took the continuation target.

Fixed structurally rather than per branch: every turn gets a stable
**arrival** number in `evaluate()` (a `Turn` value now carries wording,
chunks, `finalAt` and arrival through every routing path), `setLast()` and
the late "moved on" check compare arrival, and **one `revise()`** applies
every revision — queued coalesce, supersede, paused/late missed revision —
refreshing wording, chunks, `finalAt`, `decidedAt`, arrival and the
continuation target while keeping card identity and queue position.

6 new tests (4 scheduler, 2 engine); all 6 fail on `8f5b7a9`. Full suite
1,062 passed / 2 skipped; typecheck and build pass.

## M3 review round 7

Codex found two more edge cases:

- **Candidate speech in the question's millisecond was no barrier.** The
  candidate guards (continuation, repeat, late result) compared timestamps,
  so a confirmed candidate turn with the question's `finalAt` let the next
  follow-up rewrite the card. Candidate turns now share the arrival counter:
  the engine reserves a place with `orderMark()` when the microphone turn is
  CAPTURED and reports it via `onCandidateTurn(order)` once the echo guard
  confirms it (a dropped echo never reports). Interviewer arrival is now
  taken in `onRemoteFinal()` (a held fragment keeps its own; joined, the
  latest turn's), not after the fragment hold.
- **An older late revision rewound the continuation window.** `revise()`
  kept the newest arrival but took the older turn's `finalAt`. The time
  anchor now moves only with a newer contributing turn.

7 new tests (5 scheduler, 2 engine); all fail on `5864046` (the
capture-order one because `orderMark()` is new — it guards that a turn
captured before a question is not a barrier for it). Full suite 1,069 passed /
2 skipped; typecheck and build pass.

## M4 — conversation context and trustworthy answers

M3 accepted at `e08b684`. M4 started from the parked commit (whole turns,
`excerpt()` at render, "what you said counts as grounding") and was completed
against the board's four checks:

- **Full spoken turns stay searchable.** No clip at storage; every rendered
  block is bounded by `excerpt()`; memory is bounded per session (3 000 turns

  background; the source rule says JD/company chunks are never evidence.
- **Coaching.** `report.ts` pairs questions with captured candidate speech,
  labels suggestions separately, reports uncaptured answers instead of
  assessing them, makes no model call without speech, and bounds input per
  exchange. Sparring unchanged.

Evidence: `interview.context.acceptance.test.ts` (real engine + sql.js,
prompt captured at the provider), `report.test.ts` (real persistence), and
context/answer unit tests. Against `e08b684` 21 of the new/changed checks
fail. Full suite 1,090 passed / 2 skipped; typecheck and build pass.

## M4 review round 2

Codex reproduced four issues at `f48500c`. Fixed:

- **Unpunctuated recall** — `excerpt()` fell back to a head clip when a turn
  had no sentence breaks, so the right turn was recalled without its fact.
  Pieces longer than a window are now split into word windows and scored
  like sentences (covers run-on sentences longer than the budget too).
- **Coaching attribution** — only card rows were boundaries, so a reply to a
  missed question was credited to the card before it. Every interviewer turn
  is now a boundary (backchannel/logistics aside); an answered turn without a
  card is coached under its own words, marked not detected live; a typed Ask
  is reported as not linked.
- **Capture order** — equal spoken timestamps fell back to insertion order,
  which the echo hold reverses. New `transcript_chunks.capture_seq`
  (migration 0017), taken as each final arrives; used by `transcriptOrder()`
  and the live context; Resume continues the counter; legacy rows fall back
  to insertion order.
- **Report budget** — the per-exchange floor could exceed the declared total.
  Every request is now hard-capped and batched with stable numbering; batch
  summaries are combined in one bounded call; every question keeps its
  assessment.

10 new tests (3 real-engine, 4 report, 3 unit) fail on `f48500c`; 2 report
tests were updated for the new design (silent questions not sent; batches).
Full suite 1,101 passed / 2 skipped; typecheck and build pass.

## M6 review — four fixes (`25a1dce` → correction)

Codex returned M6 with four P2 findings, each reproduced; fixed in one
correction commit, each with a regression that fails on `25a1dce`.

1. **A failed initial Space lookup filed the session out of its Space.** The
   save prompt now has a destination state (`resolving | unknown | choose |
   known`): a failed read is UNKNOWN — Keep waits, the prompt says why and
   offers Try again or an explicit choice (a disabled "Choose where to file
   it…" placeholder, apart from an explicit No Space). The re-read before
   Keep no longer treats a failed read as "deleted" either.
2. **Retry hid its own errors and the picker.** Errors show on the result
   view; a destination confirmed gone at Retry (renderer re-read or main's
   refusal) brings the picker onto the result view and disables Retry until
   the user chooses. Retry re-runs only the failed half (`session:remember`
   `only`, the other half `not-run`), merged over the earlier result, so a
   confirmed half is neither redone nor reported away.
3. **Resume skipped the final role check.** Pressing Resume re-reads the
   session (profile, kind/mode/activity, role) and the role itself before any
   capture; a deleted role shows the deleted-role blocker, a failed read
   starts nothing. `engine.resume` refuses a missing/other-profile Space, and
   an interview with no role, before the row goes live.
4. **Saved voice quick Asks vanished from history.** `sessions.detail` keeps
   answer cards unless they are the dual-write of a detected question whose
   answer is shown; the timeline shows an answer card as a generated
   suggestion (asked → answer) and lets a linked card fill a missing answer
   instead of duplicating it.

Evidence: 10 new renderer-harness cases plus 2 updated ones fail on
`25a1dce` components (`HARNESS_ROOT`) and pass now; 8 new unit cases
(real schema / real engine) fail on `25a1dce` and pass now.

## M6 review 2 — two regressions in the correction (`f429da1` → fix)

1. **History revived an answer cleared by a paused correction.** M3's
   `surfaceMissed` revises a waiting question's wording and saves an EMPTY
   answer with the paused note; the earlier answer card (to the old wording)
   stays in `contributions`. The repo's exclusion and the timeline's fallback
   both keyed on empty text, so the old answer reappeared under the corrected
   question. Both now key on the answer ROW: a question with an answer row
   (even empty) never takes a linked card; only one with no row recovers its
   answer from the card. Quick Asks and dedupe unchanged.
2. **Choosing a Retry destination relabelled a confirmed result.** The result
   list rendered with the picker's current Space. The destination's title is
   now snapshotted with the result when it is confirmed, so "Summary saved to
   …" names where it was saved until a new result says otherwise.

Each has a regression that fails on `f429da1`: a real-persistence repo +
timeline test using the engine's own writes (plus a missing-row control), a
timeline unit case, and a hidden-renderer harness case.
