## Flexible interview fallback — scoped request

The user asked for useful answers when sources do not cover the question,
with the proposed answer remembered within the session, and testing limited
to this case. Interview prompts now answer conceptual questions from general
knowledge and give adaptable conditional wording for an unsupported personal
example. They do not infer that the candidate has or lacks experience from
silence, invent past employers/projects/results, or create citations. Missing
documents alone no longer add an interview error line. Meeting business-fact
guards remain strict.

The local session cache retains up to 200 suggested answers, each capped at
8,000 characters. Interview snapshots select the relevant proposed answer,
including older topics and typed Asks, into the existing three-lead character
budget (540 characters plus excerpt separators). The prompt ceiling does
not increase. Drafts stay labelled as AI suggestions, separate from candidate
speech, resume facts and approved memory. A replacement/cleared answer removes
its stale draft; Resume rebuilds from that session's answer records. Other
modes keep their compact lead rendering. No extra model/classifier call is
added for fallback.
