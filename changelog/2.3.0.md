# 2.3.0 — 2026-11-24

**Interviews come first.** BrainCue now opens on the interview, and the
path through one was rebuilt and tested piece by piece. That path runs from
start to detection, answers, follow-ups, history and Resume. Each role can
use its own résumé. Answers come out faster by default. When your documents
don't cover a question, you still get something useful to say.

## New

- **One Start interview.** Home leads with a single Start. The start flow
  asks for the role, then shows four rows: résumé, role and JD, audio, and
  AI. Each row has its fix if something is missing. Before you start, it
  says what will be sent to which provider. The role defaults to the one you
  last interviewed for.
- **A résumé per role.** When you create or edit a role, choose **Default
  résumé**, or upload or paste a different one. Only that role uses it.
  Saving, replacing or removing it updates what answers are grounded on.
  Prep briefs, tailoring, setup and Resume all use the role's résumé.
  Existing roles keep using your default.
- **Answers when your documents don't cover the question.** Conceptual and
  technical questions get a general-knowledge answer. A personal-experience
  question your sources can't support gets adaptable, proposed wording
  rather than a refusal or an invented story. That wording stays consistent
  within the session, including follow-ups and Resume. It is never saved as
  your history. Only real context is cited.
- **A Cue Card that stays out of the way.** The current question and its
  answer take the space. Questions that were missed stay visible, with the
  reason and an **Answer it** button. Earlier cards sit behind one toggle.
  The toolbar has evenly sized, labelled controls and a single Ask box. A
  **More** panel holds style, model, tools and grounding, and no longer
  pushes the answer off a small window.
- **Honest model labels.** The model chips say which task they control:
  **Interview answers** or **Coding solver**. They show the model the next
  request will actually use, and each answer says which model answered it.
- **History you can trust.** Saved sessions open from this computer only,
  in the order things were said, with BrainCue's suggestions marked as not
  said. Keeping a session reports what happened to its summary and to its
  memory suggestions separately, with Retry when one fails.

## Changed

- **Faster interview answers by default.** For OpenAI interview answers on
  supported GPT-6 models, **Fast processing** is on by default. It is billed
  at a higher token rate (2× Standard). Settings → Language Models →
  Advanced shows the rate and lets you turn it off. It doesn't change your
  provider, model or effort. Coding, meetings and the coding solver aren't
  affected.
- **Answers don't wait long for lookups.** A normal interview answer waits
  at most 350 ms for document and memory lookups, which usually start while
  the question is still being spoken. After that, it uses the same documents
  and allowed memories, matched by keywords. Coding keeps a longer
  allowance.
- **Questions are answered in the order they were asked,** and a corrected
  question replaces its answer instead of opening a second card.
- **Requests stay with the provider you chose.** Every feature routes by its
  task, and a retry never quietly moves your data to another provider.
- **Resume** goes through the same start checks, re-checks the role, and
  continues the same session.

## Fixed

- The role and company at the top of answers now come from the role you
  selected, including after Resume.
- An unsaved model choice in the dashboard is no longer lost when another
  setting changes, and Save writes exactly what you edited.
- Model lists fit compact Cue Cards, and dropdowns close when you click
  elsewhere.
- Tooltips stay visible when keyboard navigation scrolls the page.
- Turning memory off is checked again just before each answer request is
  sent, including memories looked up early, while the question was being
  spoken. A request already sent can't be recalled.
- Long, unpunctuated turns stay searchable after Resume.
- A document changed or removed while a question is being asked no longer
  grounds that answer on its old text.

## Known limitations

- **Answers are faster, but not yet as fast as the target.** The target,
  from the end of a question to the first useful words, is a median of
  1.5 s or less and a 95th percentile of 3 s or less. It applies to General
  and short Technical questions, each measured separately. In our synthetic
  test with Fast processing, the medians were about 2.4 s for both. The
  95th percentile was about 3.0 s for General and 4.6 s for Technical. Work
  on the remaining delay continues.
- The coding solver's time budget is a proposal, not yet measured.
