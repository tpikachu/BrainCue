# 2.1.0 — 2026-07-29

**You pick an activity, not a mode — and a Space is where a conversation is
kept.** BrainCue 2.0 widened the product past interviews; 2.1 finishes the job.
The start flow asks one question, Spaces became the unit of memory as well as of
context, and there is now a Help page and a much longer guided tour that walk you
through all of it.

## Added

- **Help & FAQ, from anywhere.** A **?** in the title bar opens a full manual: a
  quick start, what every activity does, every keyboard shortcut, and honest
  answers to the questions people actually arrive with — why nothing was
  remembered, what leaves your machine, what to do when a shortcut is already
  taken by another app.
- **A guided tour that shows you the thing.** Rewritten into four short chapters
  and fifteen steps. Each one now *navigates to the page and rings the actual
  card* rather than spotlighting the sidebar entry that leads to it, and it
  covers the consequences as well as the features — that a conversation kept
  with no Space keeps nothing, that memory does nothing until you switch it on,
  that Privacy Mode blanks your own screenshots too. Replay it from Help or
  Settings at any time.
- **Create a Space without leaving the start flow.** A **New Space** button sits
  beside the picker, pre-filled with the activity you already chose.
- **A recurring meeting in the sample data.** "Load sample data" now leads with
  a Tuesday standup — with an agenda and one finished conversation waiting in it
  — instead of three job Spaces. Keep that conversation to watch the whole
  continuity loop run on real data.

## Changed

- **When a fact changes, the old one is retired instead of competing with it.**
  A deadline that moves, a person who changes role, a decision that gets
  revisited — BrainCue used to keep both answers, and an answer grounded in
  memory could confidently give you last month's. Now a changed fact arrives in
  the review queue labelled **replaces a saved fact**, showing the value it
  would retire struck through, with a **Replace** button instead of Approve.
  Nothing is replaced without you saying so, and the old value is kept as
  history rather than deleted — it simply stops being used in answers. A fact
  merely *restated* is recognised as the same fact and no longer clutters the
  queue at all.
- **Memory can now be recalled by naming the thing it is about.** Asking about
  a ticket number, a version, a date, or a figure used to find nothing:
  retrieval was purely meaning-based, and meaning-based search blurs exactly
  those — "ATL-4471" and "ATL-4478" look nearly identical to it, though to you
  they are different tickets. Recall now also matches the specific words a
  memory contains, weighted so that a rare one counts for far more than a
  common one. A vague question still returns nothing rather than everything
  that shares a word with it.
- **One question starts every session.** The mode picker is gone. You say what
  the call *is* — meeting, project discussion, interview, study session,
  personal, game, solo — and the engine derives everything from that: what it
  listens to, when it speaks up, how it frames you, and the shape of its
  summary. Choosing a mode *and* a Space kind was the same question asked twice,
  and the two answers could disagree.
- **The Space picker only offers Spaces of the activity you chose,** because a
  Space *is* a saved activity. Picking one no longer silently rewrites the
  choice you just made.
- **A Space is now the only place a conversation is kept.** With none, nothing
  is summarised and nothing is proposed for memory — the session and its
  transcript are still saved. You are told this before the session starts, and
  offered a Space again when it ends. Interviews are the one activity that will
  not start without one: an interview is one round of several for a single role,
  and losing what was asked is losing the point.
- **Per-activity summaries.** A meeting leaves decisions and action items; an
  interview leaves what they asked and what you claimed; a study session leaves
  what landed and what did not. Verbatim quotes are checked against the
  transcript and attributed from the line they matched, so a quote can never be
  put in the wrong person's mouth.
- **Memory is its own section,** out of the Library, filterable by Space — and
  both switches now sit together on it. "Summaries of conversations" (on) and
  "long-term memory about you" (off until you allow it) used to live in two
  places under labels that both read as *remembering*.
- **One profile at a time.** A switcher above the sidebar scopes Home, Library,
  Memory, Sessions, and Insights; Profiles and Settings sit below a divider
  because they are global. The seven separate profile pickers are gone.
- **A first-run screen that starts you off,** rather than an empty app with an
  empty picker — including a "Try sample data" way in.

## Fixed

- **Home listed every profile's recent sessions** under a greeting naming one
  person. It now shows only the active profile's, and a guard fails the build if
  any dashboard surface forgets to scope a read again.
- **The first-run "Welcome" screen would not go away** after loading sample
  data, because the app never re-read its profile list when the database changed
  underneath it. The same gap left deleted profiles in the switcher after
  wiping all data.
- **The same fact was proposed for memory every single time.** A recurring
  meeting states its facts every week, and nothing checked whether you had
  already been asked — so week two re-proposed what week one approved, and
  pressing Keep twice duplicated everything. Suggestions you have already
  answered are no longer raised again.
- **Spaces no longer show a "kind" column reading *Interview* for everything.**
- Sessions and Insights showed every profile's data.
