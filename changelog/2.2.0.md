# 2.2.0 — 2026-09-07

**It answers the question the room just asked, it can transcribe without the
cloud, and it hears both sides of the call.** 2.1 made BrainCue an activity
picker; 2.2 makes the meeting seat work the way people expected it to. A
question asked in a call now gets a grounded answer on the Cue Card instead of
a held card nobody opened, transcription can run entirely on your machine, and
the model behind each cue is yours to choose, per activity, from more than one
provider.

## Added

- **Local speech-to-text.** Settings → Speech-to-Text now offers a *Local*
  engine next to the cloud one: NVIDIA's Nemotron streaming models running on
  sherpa-onnx, on your CPU, in their own process. Two models are on offer —
  *Nemotron Speech Streaming EN* (English, recommended) and *Nemotron 3.5 ASR
  Streaming* (multilingual) — each about 630 MB, downloaded from Hugging Face
  with a resumable, size-checked download. Nothing spoken leaves the machine
  while the local engine is selected, and there is no per-minute transcription
  cost. The model is loaded the moment you select it, so the first words of a
  call are not lost to a cold start.
- **A download strip under the title bar.** Any long download — today, a
  speech model — shows its progress in a slim strip just below the window's
  title bar on every page, with cancel and retry, so you can keep working
  while it fetches; a finished or failed download stays until dismissed.
- **More than one AI provider, and the best model per activity.** Settings →
  Language Models has provider tiles for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq and
  OpenRouter. Add a key to a provider and every task's dropdown lists that
  provider's whole catalog — ★ marks the models suited to the task — plus
  whatever else the key can see, fetched from the vendor automatically. Pick
  one per task (the live cue, coding, résumé tailoring, parsing, question
  classification, mock interviews), or change it on the spot from the Cue
  Card's model chip while a session runs — GPT-4.1 Mini for the live answer
  and Claude Opus 5 for the coding solver, say. A model whose provider has
  no key yet shows *Add key* and takes you straight to the right settings
  panel. Keys are stored encrypted in the main process, like the OpenAI key
  always was, and never reach the window.
- **First-run onboarding.** A fresh install now asks three things in order:
  your name, how to transcribe (cloud key or a local model, with the download
  started right there), and which AI to use. Each step is skippable; anything
  skipped shows up as a *Finish setup* checklist on Home until it is done.
- **Meetings answer questions.** In a meeting with presence set to *Balanced*
  (now the default) or *Active*, a question asked in the room becomes a
  grounded, cited answer on the Cue Card straight away — no held card, no
  extra click. *Quiet* keeps the old behaviour: a small card you can open.
  Questions bypass the meeting's interjection cooldowns, so the second
  question in a row is answered like the first.
- **The session remembers what was asked.** Every answer now sees what the
  session has already heard and answered, so "and what about the second
  option?" is answerable, and a follow-up reads against the thing it refers
  to.
- **Both sides of the call are heard.** A session captures the call's audio
  and your microphone at once, transcribes both, and labels them. Only the
  call's side can trigger a cue — your own words are kept in the transcript
  and in the session's memory, never answered back at you. If the system-audio
  picker is cancelled, the session carries on with the microphone alone and
  says so.

## Changed

- **Settings has sections.** The single long page is now sub-navigated:
  Preferences, Hotkeys, Speech-to-Text, Language Models, Privacy & Data and
  System, each reachable by its own URL, so Help, the Cue Card and the
  onboarding steps can link straight to the panel they mean.
- **The start modal no longer asks what to listen to.** *Listen to* is gone;
  the modal states what a session will hear instead. The Cue Card's settings
  lose the same dropdown and always show the microphone picker.
- **Balanced is the meeting default.** New meeting sessions start at
  *Balanced* presence; *Quiet* and *Active* remain a click away.
- **The guided tour waits for your name.** It used to start underneath the
  first-run name dialog and could not be followed; it now begins once
  onboarding is done, and can be replayed from Help at any time.
- **Node 24.** The toolchain moved to Node 24 and better-sqlite3 12 (prebuilt
  binaries, no local compiler needed). Nothing changes for installed apps.

## Removed

- **Talk to BrainCue (spoken replies) is hidden.** The push-to-talk shortcut,
  the Cue Card voice bar, the Home card and the Voice output model row are
  gone for now: in a call you do the speaking, and a generated voice playing on
  the same machine only risked leaking into your microphone. The code stays
  behind a flag.
- **The Speech-to-text row in Language Models.** Transcription engine and
  model are chosen in Speech-to-Text and nowhere else.
- **GitTensor / Bittensor material.** The subnet evaluation pipeline, its
  scripts, docs and settings are gone; BrainCue is not being published to that
  network.

## Fixed

- **Questions without a question mark are still questions.** Streaming
  transcription often finalizes a turn before the "?" arrives. "So what is the
  timeline for the launch" is now recognised on its own, without the round
  trip to the classifier that used to add several seconds.
- **Local transcripts were cut mid-sentence.** The local engine's end-of-turn
  detection fired before the model had decoded its last chunk, dropping the
  tail of a sentence. The silence thresholds now respect the model's chunk
  size.
- **The other side's words no longer appear twice.** On laptop speakers the
  microphone hears the call as well; BrainCue now drops the microphone's copy
  when the call said the same words a moment earlier. Headphones remain the
  cleanest setup, and Help says so.
- **The local engine no longer clips the first word of a turn.** After a few
  seconds of silence the local transcriber used to reset itself just as the
  next person started speaking, so "Fine, let's decide next week" arrived as
  "Let's decide next week". That reset is gone.
- **The local engine's in-flight line no longer repeats itself** ("Morning
  Morning. Have you had Morning. Have you…").
- **The first cue arrives sooner.** Retrieval now starts while the question
  is still being spoken, a Space with no documents no longer pays for an
  embedding call it cannot use, and the answer model's connection is opened
  when the session starts instead of on the first question. Measured on a
  meeting question: the wait from the end of the sentence to the first word
  on the card fell from about 4.5 s to about 2.5 s on both engines. What is
  left is the model's own first token plus the network round trip.
- **No more invented figures.** Asked something the Space has no notes on — a
  budget, a date, a decision — a meeting answer used to produce a plausible
  number. It now opens with ⚠, says the fact is not in your notes, and offers
  who or what would have it.

## Notes

- **Existing installs see onboarding once.** It is the same three steps a
  fresh install gets, every one skippable, and your existing key and profile
  are already filled in. The installer name is unchanged, so 2.2 upgrades in
  place over 2.1.
- **Two transcribers on the cloud engine** means cloud transcription cost
  roughly doubles during a call. The local engine costs nothing per minute,
  which is one more reason it exists.
- The local engine is verified on Windows x64. The sherpa-onnx runtime also
  ships macOS and Linux builds, but they have not been exercised in this
  release; the cloud engine is unchanged on every platform.
