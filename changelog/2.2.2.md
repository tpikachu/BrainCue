# 2.2.2 — 2026-10-07

**Interviews that follow the thread, and a Cue Card with less to set.** A
patch from another week of real calls on 2.2. Nothing new to learn; two
controls went away because the app can decide them itself.

## Changed

- **One answer style instead of five formats.** The Cue Card's format
  dropdown (Key points, Explanation, Detailed, Story, STAR) and the
  interview-type dropdown are gone. In their place is a single two-way chip,
  **General | Technical**: General is a bold lead sentence plus a few
  bullets, Technical is the same lead with more depth and a code block when
  one is called for. Behavioral questions get the situation → action →
  result shape automatically, because the classifier already knows a
  behavioral question when it hears one. Older formats sent by anything
  still holding them are mapped onto the two styles.
- **The end-of-session dialog no longer asks "What kind of interview was
  this?"** The session keeps the type the Cue Card used.

## Fixed

- **Follow-ups stay on the subject.** After "Tell me about your last project
  and what was your role?", the follow-up "Well, what was your role there?"
  used to get an answer about a different project. The answer prompt now
  treats a short follow-up as a continuation of the last exchange, and
  retrieval for it includes the opening of the previous answer, which is
  where the project was actually named. Verified against the live model
  with a two-project resume: three questions, no drift.
- **Your own speech shows as you say it.** The microphone side of a call
  produced no live text at all; your words appeared only after you stopped
  speaking, and then a second and a half later. Both streams now show
  interim text, one line per speaker, and the Cue Card clears the line if
  it turns out to be the call's own echo.
